The question at the heart of the book

“What is love — and where does it truly come from?”

Roger Willroth begins with a question we rarely stop to ask, then follows it back to its source. What he finds reshapes not only how we understand God, but how we see faith, life, and one another.

The Triquetra

Three in one, without beginning or end

The trinity knot — the triquetra — is a single line woven into three equal arcs: an ancient emblem of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Three persons, one God.

Its unbroken weave has no start and no finish — a fitting picture of a love that was never created and will never end. That relational, self-giving love is the very heart of God Is Love.
