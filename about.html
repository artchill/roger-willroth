About the author

Roger Willroth

A pastor's mind, a writer's heart

A theologian and author writing from the American heartland, Roger explores the questions of faith with a scholar's rigor and a pastor's warmth — always returning to a single, steadying conviction: that God is love.

Roger Willroth holds a bachelor's degree from the University of Northwestern in Roseville, Minnesota, and a Master of Divinity from Bethel University Seminary in Shoreview, Minnesota. Years of study in Scripture and theology shape everything he writes — not as dry academics, but as a lifelong search for how faith actually meets the way we live.

He resides in Ames, Iowa, where he continues to write and explore themes of faith, love, and biblical truth. His work invites readers of every background into an honest conversation — one that takes the text seriously while leaving room for wonder.

Whether examining the nature of divine love or bringing a first-century disciple vividly to life, Roger writes for the person in the pew and the seeker at the edge of it alike.
