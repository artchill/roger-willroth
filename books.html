God Is Love

Not the God You Are Imagining

Religion & Spirituality Christian Books & Bibles

What is love, and where does it come from? In this book, Roger Willroth explores the idea that love is more than an emotion — it is the very nature of God.

Through a thoughtful examination of biblical teaching, he invites readers of all faiths and backgrounds to reflect on God's relational nature and to discover how understanding divine love can transform the way we see life, faith, and one another.

Memoirs of the Beloved

A biographical narrative of the Apostle John

Genre — TBA

This book brings the life of the Apostle John to vivid life through a compelling biographical narrative. Known as “the Beloved Disciple” for his close relationship with Jesus, John shares a remarkable journey of faith, family, and discipleship.

Drawing from Scripture and historical context, Roger offers readers a fresh perspective on the man behind the Gospel of John, the Epistles of John, and the Book of Revelation.
